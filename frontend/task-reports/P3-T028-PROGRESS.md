## RBZ Required Reports

| Report | Frequency | Format |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Loan Portfolio Summary | Monthly | PDF + CSV |
| Delinquency Report | Monthly | PDF + CSV |
| Non-Performing Loans | Quarterly | PDF |
| KYC Compliance Summary | Quarterly | PDF |
| AML Suspicious Activity | As needed | PDF |
| Capital Adequacy | Quarterly | PDF |
| Interest Rate Schedule | Annual | PDF |

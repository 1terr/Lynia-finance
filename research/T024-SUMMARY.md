## Executive Summary

### Regulatory Landscape

Zimbabwe has a **structured but evolving regulatory framework** for fintech and lending businesses. Key recent developments include:

- **2021**: Cyber and Data Protection Act enacted
- **2024**: Data Protection Regulations (SI 155/2024) - Licensing deadline: **12 March 2025**
- **2025**: Banking (Payment Services) Regulations amended (SI 17/2025)

### Key Regulatory Bodies

1. **Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ)** - Banking, lending, payments
2. **POTRAZ** - Data protection, telecommunications
3. **Consumer Protection Commission** - Consumer rights
4. **Registrar of Companies** - Business registration
5. **ZIMRA** - Tax compliance

### Licensing Strategy for Lynia Finance

**Recommended Approach**: Start **without microfinance license** (operate as seller-financed credit)

**Rationale**:
- Device financing can be structured as **conditional sale agreements** (not regulated lending)
- Lower regulatory burden initially
- Faster time to market
- Option to obtain license later as business scales

**If Pursuing License**:
- Credit-Only MFI License: **USD $300 application + $20,000 minimum capital**
- Timeline: 3-6 months approval
- Ongoing supervision and reporting

## Licensing Requirements

### Option 1: Operate WITHOUT Microfinance License (Recommended Initially)

**Structure**: Conditional Sale Agreement Model

**Legal Basis**:
- Device sold on credit (not a "loan")
- Seller retains title until fully paid
- Falls under general contract law, not Microfinance Act
- Regulated by Consumer Protection Act (less onerous)

**Advantages**:
✅ No MFI license required
✅ Lower regulatory burden
✅ Faster time to market
✅ Lower capital requirements
✅ Simpler reporting

**Requirements**:
```
1. Company Registration (ZIMRA, Registrar of Companies)
2. Tax Registration (ZIMRA)
3. Data Controller License (POTRAZ) - MANDATORY
4. Consumer Protection Compliance (CPC)
5. Standard business licenses
```

**Limitations**:
❌ Cannot market as "loans"
❌ Limited interest rate flexibility
❌ May face perception issues
❌ Harder to access wholesale funding

### Option 2: Credit-Only Microfinance Institution License

**Regulatory Framework**: Microfinance Act [Chapter 24:29]

**Eligibility**:
- Incorporated company (Private Limited)
- Minimum capital: USD $20,000 (paid-up)
- Fit and proper directors and management
- Robust business plan

**Application Process**:

**Step 1: Company Incorporation** (2-4 weeks)
```
With Registrar of Companies:
• Register company (Pvt) Ltd
• Minimum 2 directors (must be Zimbabwean or have work permits)
• Minimum 2 shareholders
• Articles of Association
• Memorandum of Association

Cost: $100 - $200
```

**Step 2: Capitalize Company** (Immediate)
```
• Open corporate bank account
• Inject minimum capital: USD $20,000
• Obtain bank certificate confirming capital
• Note: Capital must be maintained throughout operation

Cost: $20,000 (capital)
```

**Step 3: Prepare Application Documents** (4-8 weeks)
```
Required Documents:

1. Application Letter (to Governor, RBZ)
2. Business Plan (detailed):
   • Executive summary
   • Market analysis
   • Products and services
   • Marketing strategy
   • Operations plan
   • Management team CVs
   • Financial projections (5 years)
   • Risk management framework

3. Corporate Documents:
   • Certificate of incorporation
   • Memorandum and Articles of Association
   • CR14 (register of directors)
   • CR6 (certificate of confirmation)

4. Financial Documents:
   • Proof of capital (bank statement)
   • Audited financials (if existing business)
   • Source of funds declaration

5. Directors and Management:
   • CVs of all directors
   • National IDs
   • Police clearance certificates
   • References (professional and character)
   • Proof of residence

6. Policies and Procedures:
   • Credit policy
   • Risk management policy
   • AML/KYC policy
   • Customer complaint handling policy
   • Data protection policy
   • IT security policy

7. Physical Infrastructure:
   • Proof of office space (lease agreement)
   • Office photos
   • IT infrastructure description

Cost: $1,000 - $3,000 (legal/consulting fees)
```

**Step 4: Submit Application** (1 day)
```
Submit to:
Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe
Bank Supervision Department
80 Samora Machel Avenue
Harare

OR Email: banksupervision@rbz.co.zw

Application Fee: USD $300
```

**Step 5: RBZ Evaluation** (3-6 months)
```
RBZ conducts:
• Document review
• Due diligence on directors/shareholders
• Fit and proper assessments
• Site inspection
• Interview with management
• Financial viability assessment

May request additional information/clarifications
```

**Step 6: License Issuance** (If approved)
```
• Conditional approval letter issued
• Pay license fee (if applicable)
• Receive license certificate
• Register with RBZ as supervised institution
• Begin supervised operations

Duration: Initial license typically 12 months, renewable
```

**Ongoing Compliance**:
```
• Quarterly financial reporting to RBZ
• Annual audited financial statements
• AML/KYC compliance reporting
• Maintain minimum capital at all times
• Supervision visits by RBZ

```

## Consumer Protection Compliance

### Consumer Protection Act [Chapter 14:44]

**Effective**: December 10, 2019

**Key Provisions Affecting Lynia Finance**:

### 1. Right to Information (Sections 26-32)

**Requirement**: Provide clear, accurate information about products/services

**For Lynia Finance**:
```
Must disclose to customer:
✓ Device price (cash and financed)
✓ Deposit amount required
✓ Loan amount (device price - deposit)
✓ Interest rate (% per annum)
✓ Monthly payment amount
✓ Loan duration (months)
✓ Total amount repayable
✓ Late payment fees
✓ Early settlement terms
✓ Default consequences
✓ Customer rights and obligations
```

**Implementation**:
```javascript
// Pre-contractual disclosure template
const loanDisclosure = {
  deviceDetails: {
    model: "Samsung Galaxy A14",
    cashPrice: "$150.00",
    condition: "Brand new"
  },

  financingTerms: {
    depositAmount: "$50.00",
    loanAmount: "$100.00",
    interestRate: "20% per annum (flat)",
    duration: "12 months",
    monthlyPayment: "$15.00",
    totalRepayable: "$180.00",
    totalInterest: "$20.00",
    APR: "36.4%" // Effective annual rate
  },

  fees: {
    latePaymentFee: "$2.00 per missed payment",
    earlySettlementDiscount: "Pro-rata interest rebate",
    deviceRecoveryFee: "$30.00 if default"
  },

  customerRights: [
    "7-day cooling-off period",
    "Early settlement option",
    "Complaint lodging mechanism",
    "Data protection rights"
  ]
};
```

### 2. Right to Fair Contracts (Sections 35-51)

**Prohibited Contract Terms**:
```
❌ Unfair terms that are:
  • Excessively one-sided
  • Hidden or unclear
  • Waive consumer rights
  • Allow arbitrary price changes
  • Exclude liability unfairly

✅ Required for valid contract:
  • Written in plain language
  • Font size minimum 10pt
  • All terms clearly stated
  • Customer signature
  • Copy provided to customer
```

**Cooling-Off Period**: **7 days** from contract signing

```
Customer Rights:
• Cancel within 7 days, no penalty
• Full refund of any payments made
• Return device in original condition

Lynia Finance must:
• Clearly state cooling-off period in contract
• Accept cancellations without question
• Process refunds within 14 days
```

**Example Contract Clause**:
```
COOLING-OFF PERIOD

You have the right to cancel this agreement within
7 days of signing without giving any reason.

To cancel, contact us at:
Phone: +263 771 234 567
Email: cancel@lynia.co.zw
WhatsApp: +263 771 234 567

If you cancel:
• Return the device in original condition
• We will refund your deposit within 14 days
• No penalties or fees will be charged

This right expires 7 days after you sign this agreement.
```

### 3. Right to Choose (Sections 18-25)

**No Forced Bundling**: Customer cannot be forced to buy additional products

**For Lynia Finance**:
```
✓ Can offer optional device insurance
✗ Cannot make insurance mandatory
✓ Can offer optional warranty extension
✗ Cannot bundle with unrelated products
```

### 4. Right to Complaint Resolution (Sections 33-34)

**Requirement**: Establish internal complaint handling mechanism

**For Lynia Finance**:
```
Must have:
1. Clear complaint procedure
2. Designated complaint officer
3. Response timeframe (14 days max)
4. Escalation path to CPC if unresolved
5. Record keeping (all complaints logged)
```

**Complaint Handling Process**:
```
Step 1: Customer Lodges Complaint
• Via WhatsApp, phone, email, or in-person
• Complaint officer records details
• Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours

Step 2: Investigation
• Review customer account
• Gather facts and evidence
• Consult relevant staff

Step 3: Resolution
• Propose solution
• Communicate to customer
• Implement if accepted

Timeline: Maximum 14 days

Step 4: If Unresolved
• Escalate to Manager
• Further 7 days for resolution

Step 5: If Still Unresolved
• Inform customer of right to CPC complaint
• Provide CPC contact details
```

### 5. Consumer Protection Commission (CPC) Interaction

**CPC Can**:
- Investigate complaints
- Issue compliance orders
- Impose penalties for violations
- Refer to court for prosecution

**Penalties**:
```
Violations can result in:
• Fines up to Level 14 (~$10,000)
• Imprisonment (serious violations)
• Business license suspension
• Restitution orders (refunds to customers)
```

**Best Practice**: Resolve complaints internally to avoid CPC involvement

## Data Protection Requirements

### Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07]

**Effective**: December 2021

**Latest Regulations**: SI 155/2024 (September 2024)

### Key Requirements for Lynia Finance

### 1. Data Controller License (MANDATORY)

**Deadline**: **12 March 2025**

**Process**:

**Step 1: Appoint Data Protection Officer (DPO)** (By 12 December 2024 - EXPIRED, but still required)

```
DPO Qualifications:
• Law, data science, IT, information systems audit, or related field
• Zimbabwean citizen or valid work permit
• No criminal record (cybercrime, fraud, etc.)

DPO Responsibilities:
• Ensure compliance with data protection laws
• Conduct data protection impact assessments (DPIAs)
• Handle data subject requests (access, deletion, etc.)
• Liaise with POTRAZ
• Train staff on data protection
• Monitor data breaches

DPO Certification:
• Complete course at Harare Institute of Technology (HIT)
• Duration: 2-4 weeks
• Cost: ~$150-$300
• Register with POTRAZ using Form DP2
```

**Step 2: Apply for Data Controller License**

```
Application via POTRAZ website:
https://www.potraz.gov.zw

Form: DP1 (Data Controller License Application)

Required Information:
• Company details (name, address, registration number)
• DPO details (name, qualifications, contact)
• Types of personal data processed
• Number of data subjects (determines tier)
• Data processing activities
• Security measures in place
• Data retention periods
• Third-party data processors (if any)
```

**Step 3: Determine License Tier**

```
Tier Classification (based on data subjects):

Tier 1: 50 - 1,000 data subjects
License Fee: USD $50/year

Tier 2: 1,001 - 10,000 data subjects
License Fee: USD $150/year

Tier 3: 10,001 - 500,000 data subjects
License Fee: USD $500/year

Tier 4: 500,000+ data subjects
License Fee: USD $2,000/year

Lynia Finance (Year 1): Likely Tier 1 or Tier 2
```

**Step 4: Pay License Fee**

```
Payment Methods:
• Bank transfer to POTRAZ account
• EcoCash/O'mari
• Cash at POTRAZ offices

Upon payment:
• License issued within 7-14 days
• Valid for 12 months
• Renewal required annually
```

### 2. Data Processing Principles

**Lawfulness of Processing**: Must have legal basis

```
Legal Bases for Lynia Finance:

1. Consent (primary basis)
   • Customer explicitly agrees to data processing
   • Consent must be freely given, specific, informed
   • Can be withdrawn anytime

2. Contractual Necessity
   • Processing necessary to perform loan contract
   • E.g., name, ID, phone needed to disburse loan

3. Legal Obligation
   • E.g., AML/KYC requirements under Banking Act
   • Tax reporting to ZIMRA

4. Legitimate Interest
   • E.g., fraud prevention, collections
   • Must balance against customer rights
```

**Data Minimization**: Collect only necessary data

```
Lynia Finance should collect:
✓ Name, ID number, phone, address (necessary)
✓ Employment details, income (for credit assessment)
✓ Next of kin (for collections)
✓ Device IMEI (for tracking financed asset)

✗ Social media passwords (excessive)
✗ Political affiliation (irrelevant)
✗ Medical history (not needed for device financing)
```

**Purpose Limitation**: Use data only for stated purpose

```
Customer told: "We collect your data to process your loan application"

Allowed Uses:
✓ Credit assessment
✓ Loan disbursement
✓ Payment reminders
✓ Collections (if default)
✓ Regulatory reporting (anonymized)

Prohibited Uses:
✗ Marketing third-party products (without separate consent)
✗ Selling data to data brokers
✗ Sharing with unrelated companies
```

### 3. Data Subject Rights

**Right to Access**: Customer can request copy of their data

```
Customer Request: "What data do you have about me?"

Lynia Finance must provide:
• Copy of all personal data held
• How it was collected
• Why it's being processed
• Who it's been shared with
• How long it will be retained

Timeline: Within 30 days
Fee: Free (or reasonable fee if excessive requests)
```

**Right to Rectification**: Customer can correct inaccurate data

```
Customer: "My address in your system is wrong"

Lynia Finance must:
• Verify correct information
• Update records within 14 days
• Notify customer of correction
```

**Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**:

```
Customer can request deletion if:
• Data no longer necessary for purpose
• Consent withdrawn (and no other legal basis)
• Data processed unlawfully

Exceptions (Lynia Finance can refuse):
• Loan still active (contractual necessity)
• Legal obligation to retain (e.g., tax records)
• Legal claims (e.g., ongoing collections case)

Timeline: Respond within 30 days
```

**Right to Object**: Customer can object to processing

```
Customer: "Stop sending me marketing messages"

Lynia Finance must:
• Stop marketing immediately
• Cannot use legitimate interest to override
• Can still send essential communications (payment reminders)
```

### 4. Data Security Measures

**Technical Measures**:
```
✓ Encryption (data in transit and at rest)
✓ Access controls (role-based permissions)
✓ Secure passwords (minimum 12 characters, complex)
✓ Two-factor authentication (for admin access)
✓ Firewall and antivirus
✓ Regular security updates
✓ Secure backups (encrypted, offsite)
```

**Organizational Measures**:
```
✓ Staff training on data protection
✓ Data protection policies and procedures
✓ Confidentiality agreements (staff)
✓ Vendor contracts (data processors)
✓ Regular security audits
✓ Incident response plan
```

### 5. Data Breach Notification

**Obligation**: Notify POTRAZ and affected customers of data breaches

**Timeline**:
```
Discovery of Breach
       ↓
Within 72 hours: Notify POTRAZ
       ↓
Without undue delay: Notify affected customers
```

**Notification Contents**:
```
To POTRAZ:
• Nature of breach
• Categories and number of data subjects affected
• Likely consequences
• Measures taken or proposed

To Customers:
• What happened
• What data was affected
• What we're doing about it
• What you should do (e.g., change passwords)
```

**Example Notification**:
```
URGENT: Data Security Incident Notification

On [date], we discovered unauthorized access to our
customer database. The breach affected [X] customers.

Data Affected:
• Names, phone numbers, ID numbers
• Loan details and payment history
• No banking details or passwords were exposed

What We've Done:
• Immediately secured the vulnerability
• Engaged cybersecurity experts
• Reported to POTRAZ
• Enhanced security measures

What You Should Do:
• Be alert for phishing attempts
• Do not share OTPs or PINs with anyone
• Contact us if you notice suspicious activity

We sincerely apologize for this incident.

Contact: security@lynia.co.zw | +263 771 234 567
```

### 6. Children's Data (Special Protection)

**Definition**: Child = under 18 years

**Requirement**: Parental/guardian consent for processing children's data

**For Lynia Finance**:
```
Policy: Do not finance devices to minors

Verification:
• Check ID age (must be 18+)
• Reject applications from under-18s

If child's data collected incidentally:
• Delete immediately

```

### 7. Cross-Border Data Transfers

**Restriction**: Personal data cannot be transferred outside Zimbabwe without:
1. POTRAZ approval, OR
2. Adequate protection in destination country, OR
3. Explicit consent from data subject

**For Lynia Finance**:
```
Likely Cross-Border Transfers:
• Cloud hosting (AWS, Google Cloud) - May be outside Zimbabwe
• WhatsApp Business API (Meta servers - international)
• Payment gateways (if international processors)

Solution:
• Use local data centers where possible
• For international services, ensure:
  - Adequate protection (e.g., EU GDPR-compliant)
  - Obtain customer consent
  - Register with POTRAZ
```

## Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and KYC

### Legal Framework

**Key Legislation**:
- Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Act [Chapter 9:24]
- RBZ AML/CFT Guidelines
- Financial Intelligence Act [Chapter 9:26]

**Regulatory Authority**: Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) - under RBZ

### KYC Requirements

**Customer Due Diligence (CDD)**: Mandatory for all customers

**Minimum KYC Information**:
```
Individual Customers:
✓ Full name
✓ Date of birth
✓ National ID number (or passport for non-residents)
✓ Residential address (verified)
✓ Contact details (phone, email)
✓ Occupation and employer
✓ Source of income
✓ Proof of income (payslip, bank statement)

Business Customers (if any):
✓ Company name and registration number
✓ Business address
✓ Directors' details (same as individual KYC)
✓ Ultimate beneficial owners (if different from directors)
✓ Nature of business
✓ Source of funds
```

**Verification**:
```
National ID:
• Inspect original document
• Verify with national ID database (if available)
• Take clear photo/scan
• Check not expired

Address:
• Utility bill (< 3 months old)
• Bank statement
• Tenancy agreement
• Visit residence (for high-value loans)

Income:
• Recent payslip
• Bank statement showing salary credits
• Employer confirmation letter
```

**Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**: For high-risk customers

```
Triggers for EDD:
• Loan amount > $500
• Customer from high-risk jurisdiction
• Politically Exposed Person (PEP)
• Unusual transaction patterns
• Customer refuses standard KYC

Additional EDD Measures:
• Source of wealth verification
• Employer verification call
• Multiple proof of address
• Background checks
• Senior management approval required
```

### Suspicious Transaction Reporting

**Obligation**: Report suspicious transactions to FIU

**What is Suspicious?**:
```
Red Flags:
• Customer provides false information
• Customer reluctant to provide KYC documents
• Transaction doesn't match customer profile
• Multiple loans in short period
• Customer asks to structure transactions to avoid reporting
• Device immediately resold after financing
• Customer uses multiple identities
```

**Reporting**:
```
If suspicious activity detected:

1. Do not inform customer (tipping off is a crime)
2. Document suspicions
3. Report to FIU within 24 hours
4. Use FIU online portal or Form STR-1
5. Continue normal business (unless FIU instructs otherwise)

FIU Contact:
Email: fiu@rbz.co.zw
Phone: +263 4 703000
Portal: https://www.rbz.co.zw/fiu
```

### Record Keeping

**Requirement**: Retain KYC and transaction records

**Retention Period**:
```
• Customer KYC documents: 5 years after relationship ends
• Transaction records: 5 years after transaction
• Correspondence: 5 years

Format: Electronic or physical (secure storage)
```

### AML Training

**Requirement**: Train all staff on AML/CFT

**Training Topics**:
```
• What is money laundering?
• Zimbabwe AML laws
• KYC procedures
• Suspicious transaction indicators
• Reporting procedures
• Penalties for non-compliance
```

**Frequency**: Annual refresher training

## Interest Rate Regulations

### Current Regulatory Environment

**No Fixed Cap**: Zimbabwe does not currently have a strict interest rate cap on microfinance loans

**RBZ Guidance**: Interest rates should be "fair and reasonable"

**Market Rates** (2024-2025):
```
Banks (personal loans): 20-35% p.a.
Microfinance institutions: 30-60% p.a.
Informal lenders: 50-120% p.a.
```

### Recommended Approach for Lynia Finance

**Transparent Pricing**:
```
Interest Rate: 20-25% per annum (flat rate)

Example:
Device Price: $150
Deposit: $50
Loan Amount: $100
Interest (20% p.a. flat): $20
Total Repayable: $120
Duration: 12 months
Monthly Payment: $10

Effective APR (calculated): ~36-40%
```

**Disclosure**: Always disclose both:
- Flat interest rate (easier to understand)
- APR/Effective rate (for transparency)

### Moneylending and Rates of Interest Act

**Historical Act**: Regulates professional moneylenders

**Key Provision**: Requires moneylending license for lending as core business

**Current Status**: Largely superseded by Microfinance Act for institutional lenders

**Relevance to Lynia Finance**:
- If operating as conditional sale: Not applicable
- If licensed MFI: Microfinance Act applies instead
- If unlicensed lending: May require moneylender license

**Recommendation**: Structure as conditional sale or obtain MFI license to avoid ambiguity

## Debt Collection Regulations

### Legal Framework

**No Specific Debt Collection Act**: Zimbabwe doesn't have dedicated debt collection legislation

**Applicable Laws**:
- Consumer Protection Act (fair treatment)
- Common law (harassment, defamation)
- Criminal law (assault, extortion)

### Prohibited Practices

**Cannot Do**:
```
❌ Harass, threaten, or intimidate debtor
❌ Use violence or threat of violence
❌ Disclose debt to third parties (neighbors, employer, family)
❌ Contact debtor at unreasonable times (before 8am, after 8pm)
❌ Misrepresent legal rights or consequences
❌ Impersonate law enforcement
❌ Trespass on debtor's property
❌ Seize property without legal authority
```

**Can Do**:
```
✓ Send payment reminders (reasonable frequency)
✓ Call debtor at reasonable times
✓ Send formal demand letters
✓ Initiate legal proceedings (court)
✓ Recover device if loan contract permits (peacefully)
✓ Report default to credit bureau
✓ Engage licensed attorney for collections
```

### Best Practices

**Professional Collections**:
```
1. Progressive escalation (gentle → firm)
2. Always respectful tone
3. Clear communication of amount owed
4. Offer payment plan options
5. Document all communications
6. Use licensed attorneys for legal action
7. Train collections staff on regulations
```

**Customer Complaint Rights**:
```
If customer complains about collection practices:
• Investigate immediately
• Respond within 14 days
• Cease prohibited practices
• Train staff to prevent recurrence
• Document resolution
```
